// Frequently asked
$ Does my child need any prior experience?
None at all. We start from zero. If they can turn on a computer, they're ready.
$ What laptop will they receive?
A refurbished Lenovo ThinkPad — durable, reliable, and perfect for learning. They'll set it up themselves during the first class and take it home after the program.
$ Is this a coding bootcamp?
No. We don't teach web development or app building. We teach how computers actually work — the operating system, command line, scripting, and troubleshooting. These are foundational skills that most CS graduates never properly learn.
$ Can adults join?
Yes. We've had strong interest from adult learners too. The registration form covers both teenagers and adults.
$ Can I pay in installments?
Yes — a deposit to secure your spot, with the balance due before the first session. Details shared upon registration.
$ What happens on Demo Day?
In the final session, students demonstrate what they've learned to parents and guests. It's a chance to see the skills in action.
